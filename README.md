## 🏎️ NTU DeepSpeed

F1TENTH / RoboRacer: 1:10 scale cars racing head-to-head on LiDAR alone, no cameras. I work on assembly, planning (the spliner) and track testing.

**Raced at** ICRA 2026 (Vienna) · **P16** at IFAC (Korea) · **P2** in the IROS sim race.

**[latifi_the_goat](https://github.com/my-usernamee/latifi_the_goat)** is my hackathon submission that got me into the team. A custom Disparity Extender on ROS2 that hits **12 m/s** on the straights and survives the chicanes without clipping a wall.

## 🌊 NTU Mecatron

Student-built autonomous underwater vehicles. I'm on the robotics software side doing **navigation and perception**, with **RobotX** coming up at the end of the year.
